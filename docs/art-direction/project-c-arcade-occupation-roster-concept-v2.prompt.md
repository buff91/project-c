Create a neutral-dark production board with six equal 96×128 guide areas and matching solid-black silhouette
previews below. From left to right show the existing player for scale, a light salvage runner, a ruptured
floor-cleaning crawler, a low wide shutter-security sentry, a slim cable-bolt thrower and a compact one-tile
Watcher. Omit labels, scenery, floor plates, baked shadows, loose props and ArcDrone.

All enemies are one masked occupation/salvage gang using hacked arcade cabinets, coin slots, ticket dispensers,
cleaners, shutters, turnstiles, CCTV and cargo loaders. Share worn charcoal metal, dirty ivory consumer plastic,
muted utility cyan, restrained amber hazards, one tiny red-orange IFF lamp and a legible split-chevron stencil.
The result must feel like improvised street technology, not professional military equipment.

- Runner: 2.5~3-head human, forward lean, oversized asymmetric pack, short powered pry baton, no shield or gun.
- Crawler: very low cleaner/vacuum base, ruptured fluid bladder and dragging hose; no eyes, legs or animal form.
- Sentry: low wide rolled-shutter armor, compact drive, dead arcade bezel, camera slit and coin-slot detail.
- Thrower: slim triangular human with an unmistakable overhead cable arc and diagonal bolt bandolier.
- Watcher: about 1.3× player mass, one-tile cargo loader with one crush arm and thin CCTV mast; no spanning wall.

Use coarse hand-authored pixel clusters, hard-edge/hard-alpha appearance, meaningful 2×2 masses, about 20~26
opaque colors per sprite and restrained highlights. Avoid anti-aliased painting, 3D micro-detail, one-pixel noise,
fantasy, supernatural/organic cores, bio-horror, bunker troops, glossy neon armor and anime mecha.

The board defines silhouette, material allocation and relative scale only. Final sprites must be redrawn as native
96×128 Aseprite sources and validated in the live B2 scene before directions or animation are commissioned.
