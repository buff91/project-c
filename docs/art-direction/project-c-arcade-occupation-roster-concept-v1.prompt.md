## Base prompt

Create a six-enemy 3×2 isometric pixel-art roster for a ruined near-future arcade complex occupied by a
scavenger faction. Show a common melee scavenger, ranged scavenger, heavy arcade-cabinet sentry, leaking
maintenance crawler, flooded-zone arc drone and a larger Warden made from prize-crane, turnstile and CCTV
parts. Each design stands on the same small isometric floor diamond with a black silhouette inset.

Unify the roster with dead CRT glass, arcade laminate, coin mechanisms, exposed cables, faded black/ochre
hazard markings and one tiny hostile red-orange sensor. All threats must have an explainable human or machine
origin. Avoid fantasy, living slime, mutations, organic anomaly cores, mascot/anime mecha and neon-flooded bodies.

## Refinement prompt

Preserve the six identities and sheet layout while correcting gameplay readability:

- Remove the melee scavenger's shield; make it a light 2.5~3-head runner with an asymmetrical salvage pack and
  short powered pry baton.
- Replace the ranged scavenger's military-gun silhouette with a raised wrist cable/bolt launcher and diagonal
  ammunition bandolier.
- Match regular human height and mass to `actor-knight`; simplify clothing into four to six material masses.
- Keep the cabinet sentry as the only regular tank and the Warden as the only substantially larger enemy.
- Simplify toward a 96×128 production plan with deliberate chunky clusters, hard edges and no micro-scratches.
- Preserve the silhouette inset, one small IFF sensor and shared faded hazard stencil.

Avoid shields on human scavengers, medieval gear, living ooze, organic cores, anime mecha, excessive detail,
floating subjects, text, logos and watermarks.
